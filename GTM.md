# Go-To-Market Strategy

### Target User
The exact target user is a **CTO, VP of Engineering, or FinOps Lead** at a Series A or Series B startup (30–150 employees). At this stage, they have significant monthly recurring API spend and seat licenses, but lack dedicated procurement departments. They are actively looking for ways to extend their runway without laying off engineers or cutting core infrastructure.

### Search Intent
Right before they’d want this tool, they are Googling queries like:
- "Cursor vs GitHub Copilot enterprise pricing"
- "How to reduce Anthropic API bill"
- "ChatGPT Team vs Enterprise plan limits"
- "Are AI coding tools worth the seat cost?"

Alternatively, they are scrolling through their monthly AWS/Stripe invoices and realizing their AI tool line-item has quietly ballooned into their top 3 SaaS expenses.

### Where They Hang Out
- **Subreddits:** r/SaaS, r/startups, r/devops, and r/fatFIRE (many successful founders idle here).
- **Slack/Discord:** Y Combinator alumni networks (Bookface), IndieHackers Slack, and specialized FinOps communities like the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) Slack.
- **X (Twitter):** Lists curating "Build in Public" founders, VC partners at firms like a16z or Sequoia who actively tweet about AI margins.

### The $0 Budget Launch Plan (First 100 Users in 30 Days)
Instead of vague content marketing, the first 30 days will rely on hyper-targeted, high-value outbound and engineered virality.
1. **The "Roast My Stack" Show HN:** Launch on Hacker News with a title like `Show HN: We built a free tool to tell you exactly how much you're overpaying for AI`. Hacker News loves cost-saving tools and pointing out inefficiencies.
2. **VC Portfolio Outreach:** Cold email partners at Seed/Series A funds with a simple pitch: *"Send this link to your portfolio founders. It will instantly tell them if they are bleeding runway on redundant AI subscriptions."* VCs are highly motivated to reduce portfolio burn and will act as distribution nodes, forwarding the link to dozens of target users simultaneously.
3. **The Viral Loop:** The tool generates a highly visual, easily shareable "Scorecard" URL. We will seed Twitter by having our own team (and friendly beta testers) post their scorecards: *"Just ran the Credex audit. Found out we were overpaying by $800/mo because we didn't realize Claude Team was overlapping with our API spend."*

### The Unfair Distribution Channel
Credex's unfair advantage is its access to **actual discounted institutional credits**. Because we have real supply, we can instantly turn an audit result into immediate savings. Anyone can build a calculator; only Credex can put a button at the end that says "Click here to claim these exact API credits at 30% off." This allows us to convert a diagnostic tool into a direct transaction.

### Week-1 Traction Targets
If this strategy works, Week 1 looks like:
- **Unique Visitors:** 1,500 (driven primarily by the HN launch and VC forwarding).
- **Audits Completed:** 600 (a strong 40% completion rate due to no upfront email gate).
- **Leads Captured:** 120 (20% of users capturing their report via email).
- **Consultations Booked:** 15 high-intent conversations generated for the Credex sales team.
