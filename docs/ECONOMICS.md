# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits.

| Assumption | Value |
|------------|-------|
| Average credit purchase | $2,000 |
| Gross margin on credits | ~20% |
| Contribution per sale | $400 |
| 12-month repeat rate | 40% |
| **LTV (12 mo)** | **$560** |

## CAC by channel ($0 paid media)

| Channel | Visitors | → Audit | → Lead | → Purchase | CAC |
|---------|----------|---------|--------|------------|-----|
| HN Show HN | 400 | 120 (30%) | 36 (30%) | ~3 (8%) | ~$0 |
| Reddit | 200 | 50 (25%) | 12 (25%) | ~1 (8%) | ~$0 |
| Credex email (warm) | 500 | 200 (40%) | 70 (35%) | ~8 (12%) | ~$0 |
| X DMs | 50 | 25 (50%) | 10 (40%) | ~1 (10%) | ~2 hrs time |

At $50/hr implied founder time, organic CAC stays **well below $100** vs $560 LTV.

## Conversion funnel (per 1,000 visitors)

```
1,000 visitors
  × 35% start audit form     = 350
  × 70% complete audit       = 245
  × 30% email capture        = 73 leads
  × 20% book consultation   = 15
  × 50% purchase credits     = 7–8 customers
```

**Margin per 1,000 visitors:** 7.5 × $400 = **~$3,000 contribution**

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR ≈ **$83k/month gross credit sales**.

At $2,000 average purchase and 40% repeat within 12 months, you need roughly **42 new credit purchases/month** sustained.

| Phase | Months | Visitors/mo | Purchases/mo | Approx. MRR (credits) |
|-------|--------|-------------|--------------|------------------------|
| Launch | 1–3 | 1,000 | 7 | ~$14k |
| Traction | 4–6 | 3,000 | 22 | ~$44k |
| Scale | 7–12 | 6,000 | 45 | ~$90k |
| $1M run rate | 13–18 | 12,000 | 90 | ~$180k MRR → **$1M+ ARR** |

**What has to be true:**

1. Credex converts consultations at **50%+** (if 20%, double top-of-funnel or improve sales motion).
2. Credex customer email + one HN hit drive **months 1–6**; SEO + shares compound **months 7–18**.
3. Audit → email → consult automation stays **< 48hr** follow-up for high-savings leads.

**Break scenario:** consultation conversion drops to 20% → need ~10,000 visitors/month instead of 6,000, or raise average purchase to $3k+.
