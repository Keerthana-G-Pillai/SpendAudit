## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free? What is the catch?**
A: Yes, completely free. No credit card, no account required. The audit runs instantly in your browser. The only ask is an optional email at the end if you want a copy sent to you — and we only ask after you have seen the results.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: All pricing data is verified weekly from official vendor pricing pages, every number links to a source. Recommendations are based on published plan features and your team size — not guesses or generalizations.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: Your audit is saved so you can share it via a unique link. We do not store any payment information. If you enter your email, it is used only to send your audit summary — never sold or shared.

**Q: What is Credex and why is it in my audit?**
A: Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits sourced from companies that overforecast. If your audit shows significant savings, Credex may be able to help you capture even more. We surface it only when it is genuinely relevant — not for everyone.

**Q: What AI tools do you cover?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf — covering the tools most startups are actually paying for, with pricing updated weekly.
