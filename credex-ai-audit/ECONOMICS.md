# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Assumptions:
- Average credit purchase: $2,000 (conservative — companies buying in bulk likely spend more)
- Gross margin on credits: ~20% (sourced from companies that overforecast, so Credex buys at discount)
- Contribution per sale: $400
- Repeat purchase rate in 12 months: 40% of buyers repurchase at least once
- LTV (12-month): $400 + (40% × $400) = $560 per customer

So a converted lead (audit → consultation → purchase) is worth approximately **$560 LTV**.

## CAC at each channel

| Channel | Est. reach | Conversion to audit | Conversion to lead | Conversion to purchase | CAC |
|---------|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|----------------------|-----|
| HN Show HN | 400 visitors | 30% = 120 audits | 30% = 36 leads | 8% = ~3 purchases | $0 / ~$0 CAC |
| Reddit posts | 200 visitors | 25% = 50 audits | 25% = 12 leads | 8% = ~1 purchase | $0 / ~$0 CAC |
| Credex customer email | 500 warm contacts | 40% = 200 audits | 35% = 70 leads | 12% = ~8 purchases | $0 / ~$0 CAC |
| Direct DMs on X | 50 contacts | 50% = 25 audits | 40% = 10 leads | 10% = ~1 purchase | ~2 hrs time |

At $0 paid budget, CAC is effectively time cost only. Even at $50/hr implied time cost, acquiring a $560 LTV customer through organic channels is strongly positive.

## Conversion funnel math

```
Visitors → Audit started → Audit completed → Email captured → Consultation booked → Purchase

1000 visitors
  × 35% start form       = 350 started
  × 70% complete audit   = 245 completed
  × 30% email capture    = 73 leads
  × 20% book consult     = 15 consultations
  × 50% purchase         = 7–8 purchases
```

Revenue per 1000 visitors: 7.5 × $2,000 = **$15,000 gross / $3,000 margin**

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR = ~$83,000/month in credit sales

At $2,000 average purchase and 40% repeat rate:
- Need ~42 new purchases/month from the tool
- At 7–8 purchases per 1,000 visitors: need ~5,500–6,000 visitors/month to the audit tool
- At current organic estimates: achievable by month 6 if Credex sends the tool to its existing network and one HN post hits

More realistic 18-month model:

| Month | Visitors/mo | Purchases/mo | Cumulative ARR |
|-------|------------|-------------|---------------|
| 1–3 | 1,000 | 7 | $14k MRR |
| 4–6 | 3,000 | 22 | $44k MRR |
| 7–12 | 6,000 | 45 | $90k MRR |
| 13–18 | 12,000 | 90 | $180k MRR → ~$1M ARR run rate |

**Key assumption that must be true:** Credex's sales team converts consultations at 50%+. If that drops to 20%, the model breaks and the funnel needs more volume or a higher-touch follow-up sequence.
