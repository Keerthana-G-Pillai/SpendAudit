### FAQ
**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. 100% free. We don't ask for a credit card, and we don't even ask for your email until after we show you your audit results.

**Q: How does Credex make money?**
A: If your audit reveals massive overspend (>$500/mo), we offer to sell you pre-purchased, discounted AI API credits to capture those savings. If your savings are small, the tool is just a free utility for you.

**Q: Do you read my API keys or source code?**
A: No. We do not integrate with your codebase or require API keys. You simply input your current subscription tiers and seat counts manually.

**Q: Is the pricing data accurate?**
A: Yes. Our engine is hard-coded with the latest pricing tiers from major vendors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, etc.) and is updated weekly.

**Q: What if we have negotiated enterprise rates?**
A: Our calculator uses public retail pricing as a baseline. If you have custom negotiated rates, your actual savings might differ slightly, but the overlap and seat-efficiency recommendations will still apply.
